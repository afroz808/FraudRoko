FraudRoko Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

FraudRoko is a cyber-safety assistant that helps users understand online fraud, scams, and device-security risks. FraudRoko is not an antivirus.

Information we use

  • Account information: email address and authentication information when you create or use a FraudRoko account.
  • Device security information: information needed to perform the Phone Security Scan, such as Android device and security settings and installed-app information.
  • Notification-access security information: the Phone Security Scan can inspect which apps currently have Android Notification Access enabled. FraudRoko does not use that check to read your private notification content.
  • Security scan and entitlement data: the app stores the local free-scan counter on the device, with two free phone scans per installation. Premium entitlement status is stored server-side after Google Play verification.
  • Purchase information: Google Play purchase information needed to verify Premium subscriptions. FraudRoko does not process the user's Google Play payment card details.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: Firebase services may process app usage or diagnostic information used to operate and improve the service.

How we use information

  • To provide account and authentication features.
  • To perform the Phone Security Scan and show security reports.
  • To enforce free-scan limits and Premium subscription access.
  • To provide notifications, cyber-safety content, learning features, and support.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and improve reliability.

Installed apps

FraudRoko uses Android installed-app visibility for its core Phone Security Scan. Installed-app information is used for security analysis and is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale.

Third-party services

FraudRoko uses services such as Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, and other service providers required by particular features. Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Payments and subscriptions

FraudRoko Premium subscriptions on Android are purchased through Google Play Billing. The current Premium plans are ₹99/month and ₹999/year when those prices are configured in Google Play. Google Play controls the checkout and payment processing.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to provide FraudRoko features, security, authentication, analytics, notifications, or subscription verification.

Account deletion

You can request deletion through the account-deletion option in the FraudRoko app. You can also use the public account-deletion resource provided by FraudRoko. Account-related data is deleted unless specific information must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or legal reasons.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy or account-deletion questions, contact fraudroko.support@gmail.com.
