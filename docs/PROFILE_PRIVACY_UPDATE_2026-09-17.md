# FraudRoko Profile / Privacy Update — 2026-09-17

## Updated
- Profile About section is localized through AppText for English, Hindi, and Marathi.
- Privacy Policy content now explains account information, installed-app inventory, device security information, notifications, link scanning, analytics, third-party services, data deletion, and permissions.
- Terms & Conditions content is localized and explains security-result limitations and third-party services.
- Permissions are explained in simple language:

  - POST_NOTIFICATIONS — notifications when the user allows them.
  - QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — installed-app visibility required by the Phone Security Scan.
  - MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / All Files Access — not used by the active Android manifest.
- Account deletion remains available in Profile.

## Policy note
The in-app disclosure does not replace the required public Privacy Policy URL, Play Console Data Safety declaration, or QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Permissions Declaration. Those must be completed in Play Console using the final deployed implementation.
